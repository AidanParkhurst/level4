Trivia fundraising

Fundraisers people actually want to join

Level4's trivia model uses attention, rivalry, and community pride to drive donation momentum. Instead of simply asking supporters to give, organizations invite them into a competitive experience with a clear prize, a live leaderboard, and a cause worth backing.

How the model works

01

Choose the theme and prize

Organizations choose a theme for their audience, and offer a prize, ranging from merchandise and tickets to one-of-one experiences.

02

Promote it to supporters

Fans join with a link or QR code, and pay to compete. The event spreads through social channels, supporter communities, and word of mouth.

03

Engagement raises funds

Entry fees generate revenue, while the live competition format makes the experience social, sharable, and repeatable.

Supporter energy People participate because it feels fun, social, and competitive.
Flexible audiences Use cases already span charities, athletic clubs, and fan communities.
Simple rollout Teams promote tournaments through existing club, chapter, or foundation channels.
Clear outcome Organizers get a fresh fundraising tool with a measurable participation loop.
